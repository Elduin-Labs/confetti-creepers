# Confetti Creepers

<img src="src/main/resources/assets/icon.png" width="128" align="right">

A silly mod by Elduin. Creepers don't blow up anymore. They explode into **confetti**!

- **Confetti pop.** When a creeper goes off, there's no boom and no hole. Just a giant cloud of confetti and fireworks.
- **Confetti Guy.** If you're close when it pops, you get covered in confetti and become Confetti Guy. Confetti falls off you every step you take, over and over and over, for 5 minutes.
- **Creeper calls.** Creepers are on the Wi-Fi now. It's called *Minecraft World*. When a creeper starts hissing, it calls you: "Ssssss... WHAT THE @#$%&?!"

Works with Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.2 on Fabric. You also need Fabric API.
